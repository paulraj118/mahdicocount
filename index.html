🌴 Who We Are

Trusted in the Coconut Trade

Mahdi Coconuts & Agro Products is a GST-registered agro-commodity business based in Periyakulam, Theni District, Tamil Nadu. With over 3 years of experience, we have built a trusted supply chain connecting farmers, traders, factories, and exporters.

We maintain large stocks of fresh green coconuts and dry mature coconuts (kasangal), ensuring uninterrupted supply with skilled grading, packing, and logistics.

From semi-husked coconuts for metro markets to export-grade kasangal for the Middle East — we deliver consistency and quality every time.
